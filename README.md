## What it is

One HTML file. No libraries, no network calls, no `Math.random` — every star, stone, and
shadow is computed from seeded mathematics. You open it in a browser and you are standing
in open cyberspace before the black hole at t0/φ. Everything else, you summon.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (or visit the GitHub Pages URL once enabled).
Click **ENTER THE OPEN SPACE**, then click the canvas to take the pointer.

## What lives inside

- **The Chain** — every act is hash-anchored (cyrb53), persisted, verifiable on boot,
  exportable and replayable. ALL SPACE IS REMEMBERED.
- **The Library** — five codexes (cosmic dust · 36 gems · 38 alloys · 45 ores) plus the
  full 118-element table, with true densities, melt/boil points, flash points,
  radioactivity, crystal systems, and thermal expansion. Two entries removed by the
  Architect: *Biological Organism* and *Mana*.
- **True Forms** — every substance is drawn as its true structure: lattice parameter
  a = (n·M / ρ·N_A)^⅓ — BCC, FCC, HCP, diamond-cubic, the B₁₂ icosahedron; gem habits,
  amorphous glass, THMM dust shards, ore veins.
- **LAW·QUANTA** — four glyphs beneath everything, each carrying its 0–9 link scale read
  from the substance's own density, seated on the 24-step dial — even the black hole is
  built from the grammar.
- **LAW·SCALE·DESCENT** — nine levels, each a re-based 0–9 world, each 10⁴ in the meter,
  down to the codex floor at ~1.6×10⁻³⁵ m, where the numbers invert into the quantum field.
- **The VW OQ SEED v2.2 core** — the 24-beat clock at the derived dial 1000/7.83 ms;
  pairs with their introverted twins (always computing, never rendered); Möbius crossings
  that bleed, never cut; the tunnel on the odd band 5·7·9; the cosmic HEART↔FIELD cycle.
- **The Bloom (Decision Organ)** — resonance as normalized alignment; a chamber of
  candidates; the coherent one blooms.
- **Λ rooms** — walls meet at nine; the written doors (TREE OF TRUTH · TREE OF
  DISTORTION · the trinity · the seal in the door alphabet); gravity inside, free flight
  outside; the console with true five-light illumination and shadows on all six faces.
- **The Tray** — the portable controller: family-grouped materials, seven motions,
  deterministic mixing, apply to a room or an open-space field.
- **The audit suite** — deterministic URL fixtures (`?demo=1` `?warp=` `?vfly=` `?gqv`
  `?mu` …) let any reviewer replay and verify every system.
